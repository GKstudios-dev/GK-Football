Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Welcome to GK Football — your home for live scores, fixtures, league standings, and football news. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use our app and website.

Key Point: Following matches, live scores, league tables, and news on GK Football never requires an account, and we collect nothing from you to do that. Creating a free account (only needed to save Favorite Teams, like articles, or leave comments) collects an email address and a username you choose — we do not collect, see, or store your password.

1. Information We Collect

You can browse everything in GK Football — live scores, fixtures and results, league tables and standings, and every article across club news, national team news, transfers, and breaking news — without creating an account or providing any personal information.

If you choose to create an account (only required to save Favorite Teams, like an article, or leave a comment), we collect:

  • Email address — used to sign you in. This is handled by Firebase Authentication (a Google service); we never see or store your password ourselves.
  • Username — the display name you choose at sign-up. This is what appears next to any comment you post, instead of your email.

Depending on how you use your account, we also store:

  • Favorite Teams — the teams you choose to follow, linked to your account, so we can show them pinned at the top of Matches and personalize what you see there.
  • Article likes — which articles you've liked, linked to your account, so we can show your like back to you and prevent duplicate likes.
  • Comments — the text of any comment you post, along with your username and the time it was posted.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your email address solely to sign you in and secure your account. We use your username to identify your comments to other readers. We use your Favorite Teams, likes, and comments only to personalize and display them back within the app and website — we do not use this information for advertising profiling, and we do not sell it to anyone.

3. Passwords

We do not collect or store your password. Account sign-in is handled entirely by Firebase Authentication (a Google service), which stores passwords in encrypted form on Google's infrastructure — GK Football itself never has access to your password in readable form.

4. Match, Score, and League Data

The Matches, Live, and Leagues tabs are powered by third-party sports-data providers, which supply fixtures, live scores, match statistics, and league standings. Looking up this data does not involve sending any of your personal information to those providers — we simply request public football data and display it back to you. Favorite Teams selections are stored only in our own systems (see Section 1) and are not shared with these data providers.

5. Data Storage

We use Firebase (a Google service) as our cloud backend — specifically Firebase Authentication for account sign-in, and Cloud Firestore to store articles, your username, your Favorite Teams, your likes, and your comments. Firebase may also collect anonymous usage data for analytics, which is governed by Google's own privacy policies.

6. Comments Are Public

Any comment you post is visible to every reader of that article, shown alongside the username you chose. Please don't include personal information you wouldn't want made public in a comment. You can delete your own comments at any time; we (the app owner) can also remove comments that violate our Terms of Use.

7. Advertising

GK Football is free to use and is supported by advertising for readers who have not purchased Premium. Ads are served through Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads), which may use device identifiers and app usage information, together with your activity on other apps and sites, to personalize the ads you see. Where required by law (for example, for users in the EEA and UK), we obtain your consent before showing personalized ads, using Google's own consent tools; you can review or change this choice at any time from within the app. Outside those regions, you can still opt out of ad personalization at any time through your device's own settings. This data collection and use is governed by Google's own privacy and advertising policies, not by GK Football directly. Purchasing Premium (see Section 8) removes ads from the app entirely.

8. Premium (In-App Purchases)

GK Football offers an optional Premium upgrade (removes ads and unlocks extra features), purchased through Google Play Billing. Your payment is handled entirely by Google Play — we never see or store your card details or other payment information. We only receive confirmation from Google Play of whether your Premium purchase is active, which we use to remove ads and unlock the relevant features for your account.

9. Third-Party Services

Our app and website use the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Authentication (by Google) – for account sign-in
  • Cloud Firestore (by Google) – for storing articles, usernames, Favorite Teams, likes, and comments
  • Sports-data providers – for live scores, fixtures, results, and league standings shown in Matches, Live, and Leagues
  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads) – for displaying ads to users who have not purchased Premium
  • Google Play Billing – for processing optional Premium purchases

These services have their own privacy policies, which you can review on their respective websites.

10. Your Rights

You can delete any comment you've posted, or change your Favorite Teams, at any time from within the app or website. If you'd like your account and associated data (email, username, Favorite Teams, likes, comments) deleted entirely, contact us using the details below and we'll process that request.

11. Children's Privacy

GK Football is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. In line with Google AdMob's own policies, we do not knowingly serve personalized advertising to children under 13. If you believe a child has created an account, please contact us so we can remove it.

12. Future Updates

If we add features that change what data we collect, we will update this Privacy Policy and note the change here.
